POC #7 — Evidence Rolling-Buffer Recording

Standalone R&D prototype. Video is processed and recorded in this browser tab; a short clip is saved to this machine's local disk only (a clips/ folder next to this POC, gitignored) when you simulate an alert — never uploaded anywhere external. Not wired to any production app. See README.md for the test matrix and an important note on deleting your local clips when you're done testing.

What this does & how to test it
  • This records a short video clip automatically whenever a proctoring alert would fire — a few seconds before and after the moment it happened — instead of recording your whole session the entire time.
  • Click Start camera, wait a few seconds for the buffer to fill, then click any "Simulate alert" button below.
  • Wait for the capture to finish (a few seconds) — a clip should appear in the list at the bottom that you can play back.
  • The clip should show what was happening in the moments just before and after you clicked, not just the moment itself.
  • This POC saves real video to your own computer's disk (unlike POC #1–4, which only ever logged text). Use Clear all clips below when you're done testing.
