POC #1 — Face Presence / Out-of-Frame / Multi-Face Detection

Standalone R&D prototype. All inference runs locally in this browser tab via MediaPipe (WASM) — no video frame is ever sent to a server. Not wired to any production app. See README.md for how to run the test matrix and where results get recorded.

What this does & how to test it
  • This watches your webcam and flags when your face isn't properly visible: you leave the camera view, sit too far away, cover your face, or a second person appears.
  • Click Start camera, then try: stepping out of view, moving very close/far, briefly covering your face, holding still out of frame for a few seconds, and having someone else step into frame.
  • Watch the status message above the video and the event log below — each row shows what was detected and for how long.
  • A very quick, split-second change (e.g. a fast hand wave) should not log an event — only sustained issues should.
