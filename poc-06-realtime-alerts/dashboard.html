POC #6 — Proctor Dashboard

Standalone R&D prototype. Live feed of proctoring alerts, for a human to review — AI flags, a person decides. See README.md for the test matrix.

What this does & how to test it
  • This is the proctor's view. Open candidate.html in another tab or window to send it simulated alerts.
  • Each alert needs a decision: Acknowledge it first, then Confirm, Dismiss, or mark False positive — the AI never decides on its own.
  • The time shown on each card is how long it took to arrive after being sent — that's the thing this POC is really testing.
  • Try opening this page in two browser tabs at once — both should receive the same alerts and stay in sync when either one reviews an event.
