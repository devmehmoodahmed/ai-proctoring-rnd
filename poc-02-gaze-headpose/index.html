POC #2 — Gaze / Head-Pose ("Looking Away") Detection

Standalone R&D prototype. All inference runs locally in this browser tab via MediaPipe Face Landmarker (WASM) — no video frame is ever sent to a server. Not wired to any production app. See README.md for how to run the test matrix and where results get recorded.

What this does & how to test it
  • This watches your webcam and flags when you turn your head away from the screen — for example checking a second monitor, looking down at notes, or looking away for a while.
  • Click Start camera, then look straight at your screen and click Calibrate — this tells the app what "looking at the screen" looks like for you, so it works no matter where your webcam is positioned.
  • Then try: turning your head left/right, looking down/up, glancing away briefly, and staying turned away for a few seconds.
  • Watch the status message above the video and the event log below — each row shows what was detected and for how long.
  • A quick, brief glance away should not log an event — only sustained looking-away should.
